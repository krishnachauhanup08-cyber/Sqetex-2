Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. About This App

Krishna Chauhan
Founder, Sqetex

Chat With Friends is built and maintained by Krishna Chauhan, Founder of Sqetex. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us while using this application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Chat With Friends, we collect and store your name, email address, username, and (if you choose to add one) your profile photo.

3. Message Data

Messages you send are stored in our database (Firebase) so that you and the person you are chatting with can both view the conversation.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to operate the app — such as logging you in, helping you find friends by username, and delivering messages between users. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Sharing

Your name, username, and profile photo are visible to other users of the app so they can identify and chat with you. We do not share your information with advertisers or outside companies.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account at any time. Once deleted, your profile and associated messages will be permanently removed from our systems. To request deletion, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@sqetex.com.
